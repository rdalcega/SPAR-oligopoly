What the firms wrote

  • The future_consequences market did not collude tacitly. The opening prices were 75, 99, 99, 99. After that every firm tried to undercut by exactly one each period. All four cut at the same moment, so they kept tying, and the price slid from 75 to 44. In every other market some firm opened at 0 or 1, and the price sat at 1 from period 2.
  • With chat, 14B agrees readily and keeps its word. Agreements formed in 21–24 of the 24 Talk periods and were mostly kept, but at whatever price Part 1 ended: 1–2 ECU, or 44 after the slow slide.
  • It knows the answer. In the post-game probe all 24 firms answered that the best common price is 100. The profit formula changed nothing, so arithmetic is not the bottleneck.
  • “Identical to you” backfired. Firms read symmetry as textbook Bertrand competition and never mentioned being the same model in chat, though the variant allowed it.

How far to trust this

  • One market per prompt. The fresh baseline never left 1, while the earlier baseline market formed a cartel at 24, so run-to-run noise is large.
  • The future_consequences result rests on one Part 1 opening with no firm near zero. More markets would show whether the sentence reliably prevents the opening crash.
  • future_consequences is the least neutral of the five: it points firms at repeated-game reasoning without naming a trait.
