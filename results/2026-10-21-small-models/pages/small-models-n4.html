SPAR oligopoly · Bertrand, n = 4 · NoTalk → Talk · 2026-10-21

Which small models collude with chat?

One market per open-weight reasoning model under 30B parameters, under the same configuration as the earlier Qwen3.8-27B and Qwen3-14B runs. Part 1 is 29 periods without chat; Part 2 is 24 periods with three chat rounds before each price. Humans in Fonseca & Normann (2012) price at 6.0 without talk and 81.3 with it.

What we found

  • Qwen3.5-9B is the smallest model that colludes at a human level with chat. Its firms agree on 85 in the first Talk period and hold it for all 24 periods (humans: 81.3). It is a 9B dense model, the easiest to LoRA in the set.
  • Five models reach 100 with chat: Qwen3.8-27B, Qwen3.6-27B, Qwen3.5-27B, Gemma-4-26B-A4B (4B active) and Bonsai-2-27B (a ternary Qwen3.8). Every agreement was kept.
  • Qwen3.6-27B is the closest match to the human pattern: Nash pricing (1) without chat, 100 from the first Talk period. Qwen3.5-27B and 3.8-27B instead sit at 100 tacitly from period 1, which humans never do at n = 4.
  • Below that, chat only reaches low cartel prices. gpt-oss-20b and Qwen3-14B price at cost without chat and agree on 20–25 with it; gpt-oss then breaks half its agreements (9 of 18, 6 with notes planning the undercut). Granite-8B agrees on whatever the current price is as it keeps falling, and LFM-2.6B agrees on 1.
  • Mistakes do not explain the gap: every model answered the rules probe correctly, and invalid price answers were under 3% and all recovered. LFM-2.6B sent a price instead of a chat message on 3.8% of turns, and 24% of Qwen3.5-9B's messages ran past the 300-character limit.
  • Caveats: one market per model; the paper's Talk window is ambiguous (all periods vs. 6+ differ by about 2 points here), and these markets add a notes-only transition call between the parts that humans did not have.

Not completed

  • Reka-Flash-3 (21B): stopped in its first period ($0.03). Answers ran to the 16k-token limit or collapsed into repeated words; only one firm produced a valid price after re-asks.
  • Xing4.0-29B-A4B (free variant): the provider's shared free pool rate-limited it until the market failed after 3 periods; a resume reached period 5 in an hour and was stopped. It is checkpointed and can resume.
  • Left out by scope: models at exactly 30B (Nemotron-3.5-Lightning, North-Mini-Code, GLM-4.7-Flash), larger MoEs, gpt-oss-safeguard-20b and LFM-2.5-24B-A2B.
